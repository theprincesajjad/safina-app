about

each morning safina. gives you a single saying of the Prophet ﷺ or the Imams of his family (a), chosen for something you actually live through: before you speak, with your family, when you worry, at the end of a long day at work. a small hand-drawn picture sits beside it, and each day has its own colour, from the sea.

nothing is cut down. every hadith is the complete statement, from al-Kafi, graded reliable by al-Majlisi and kept by al-Behbudi in his Sahih al-Kafi, with its number as on thaqalayn.net beside it.

  • one hadith a day, with its reference
  • a hand-drawn picture for each one, or draw your own
  • widgets for your home screen
  • share cards for posts and stories, signed safina.
  • no ads, no account, nothing tracked

the hadith are free, always. if safina. keeps you company, you can keep the boat afloat: a one-time supporter unlock adds more widgets, a widget colour of your own, a gallery of past days and your drawings, and a small surprise behind the name. or leave a small tip.

the name

safina (سفينة) is Arabic for ship. the Prophet ﷺ likened his family, the Ahl al-Bayt, to the ark of Nuh: whoever boards it is saved.

privacy

safina. collects no data. there are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. nothing you do in the app leaves your phone.

the pictures you draw, the colours you pick and the days you open the app are saved only on your device, so the app and its widgets can show them. deleting the app deletes them.

when you share a card, safina. asks to add it to your photos so you can post it. it only adds; it never reads your library. the card goes only where you send it.

a gentle shake of the phone rocks the page. the motion sensor is read on your phone while the app is open and is never stored or sent anywhere.

purchases (the supporter unlock and tips) are handled entirely by Apple. safina. never sees your payment details.

support
